## How we measure

Before we build, we record how long the process takes today, how often it runs and where errors happen. After go-live, the orchestrator logs every run, so the same numbers can be compared. That is how “2 hours to 17 minutes” and “6 hours a week” were measured.

> If an automation won’t save more than it costs, we tell you before you pay for anything.
