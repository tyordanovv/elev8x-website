## What we automate for carriers

- **[Order intake](/solutions/order-intake/):** orders from any format posted into your TMS, with unclear orders sent back with the missing fields named.
- **[Invoice checks](/solutions/invoice-checker/):** every invoice matched to orders, rates and proofs of delivery before payment.
- **Customer status updates:** customers informed at set points, without anyone typing emails.
- **[Freight enquiries](/solutions/freight-dispatcher/):** for carriers who also broker loads, enquiries and quotes handled within your limits.

## What changes for the business

- **Office work scales with your fleet without new hires** for routine tasks.
- **Fewer errors in orders and invoices**, because every record is checked before it is posted or paid.
- **Fewer “where is my load?” calls**, because customers are informed before they ask.
- **One morning report** from the digital worker instead of checking every queue by hand.
