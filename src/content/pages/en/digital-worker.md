## What it means for the business

- **One short report instead of five dashboards.** Managers read what was decided and what needs them, in plain language.
- **Problems are caught when they happen**, not when a customer complains.
- **Every decision can be traced**: the input, the rule or model that decided, the outcome and who approved it.
- **Growth without more supervision.** New automations report to the same digital worker, so the tenth one takes no more of your managers’ time than the first.

## It coordinates AI agents too

On [Autolensio](/case-studies/autolensio/), our own data platform for car dealers, the digital worker runs the whole path from feedback to release. After a customer call, AI drafts tasks and defects that the customer manager accepts or adjusts. The digital worker picks up approved tasks that meet the criteria, a developer agent writes the fix, a QA agent tests it, and the change is deployed. People decide what gets built, and the agents do the routine work.

## What it watches

Runs, queues, errors, response times and limits, plus anything that looks unusual compared with a normal day. When something breaks, the right person hears about it with the context to act, and the manager sees it in the next report.
