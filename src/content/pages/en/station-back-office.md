## What changes for the business

- **6 hours a week back**, measured at a multi-site operator. At that pace that’s more than 300 hours a year.
- **One summary for all sites** every morning, instead of collecting reports station by station.
- **Differences caught the same day.** Cash and stock gaps are flagged while they are still easy to explain.
- **Deliveries reconciled automatically** against orders, including paper delivery notes.

## Built for multi-site operators

The more sites you run, the more the paperwork multiplies. The digital back office applies the same checks to every station, so adding a site adds no back-office hours.
