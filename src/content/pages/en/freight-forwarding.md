## What we automate for forwarding teams

- **[Freight enquiries and carrier quotes](/digital-roles/freight-dispatcher/):** requests read, carriers asked, quotes compared and booked within your limits.
- **[Order intake](/digital-roles/order-intake/):** orders from email, PDF and Excel posted into your TMS without retyping.
- **[Invoice checks](/digital-roles/invoice-checker/):** carrier invoices matched to orders and agreed rates before payment.

## What changes for the business

- **More enquiries with the same team.** Each one needs 17 minutes of attention instead of 2 hours.
- **Faster quotes to customers**, which wins loads that go to whoever answers first.
- **Margins protected**, because bookings stay inside your price limits.
- **Fewer overpayments**, because every invoice line is checked against the agreed rate.

## Built around your systems

Your TMS, your mailbox, freight exchanges such as TIMOCOM, and the Excel files your team already uses. Customer and carrier data stays on EU servers under a GDPR data processing agreement.
