## What changes for the business

- **No more retyping.** Clean orders go straight into your system, so your team stops copying data between screens.
- **Fewer errors downstream.** Addresses, dates, weights and prices are checked before an order is posted, not after a truck is booked.
- **No chasing.** Unclear orders go back to the sender with the missing fields named.
- **Peaks without overtime.** Monday mornings and month-ends are handled at the same speed as a quiet day.

## Where it fits

Order intake is often the first role we automate, because it feeds everything after it: dispatching, invoicing and customer updates. It pairs well with the [digital freight dispatcher](/digital-roles/freight-dispatcher/) and the [digital invoice checker](/digital-roles/invoice-checker/).
