## What we automate for fuel-station groups

The [digital station back office](/digital-roles/station-back-office/) consolidates the daily reports from every station, reconciles fuel deliveries against orders, flags cash and stock differences the same day and sends the owner one summary every morning.

## What changes for the business

- **6 hours a week back** at a multi-site operator, measured against the old routine.
- **Problems found while they are still fresh.** Differences are flagged the same day, not at month-end.
- **One view of all sites** each morning, without phone calls or spreadsheets.
- **Growth without more paperwork.** Each new site gets the same checks automatically.
