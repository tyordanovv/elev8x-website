## The problem

Every site produced its own daily reports, delivery notes and cash figures. Bringing them together, booking every fuel delivery into the system, recording the cash collections, reconciling deliveries against orders and spotting cash or stock differences took hours of manual work each week, and differences were often found late.

## What we built

A digital station back office for both sites:

- **Code** consolidates the daily reports, books fuel deliveries into the system, records the cash collections, reconciles deliveries against orders and flags differences.
- **AI** reads paper and PDF delivery notes, so nothing has to be typed in.
- **The owner** receives one summary for all sites every morning and keeps the decisions on suppliers, staff and sites.

## The result

- **6 hours a week back**, measured against the routine before the build. At that pace, more than 300 hours a year.
- **Fuel deliveries and cash collections recorded automatically**, with nothing typed in by hand.
- **Cash and stock differences flagged the same day**, while they are still easy to explain.
- **One picture of the business every morning**, across both sites.
