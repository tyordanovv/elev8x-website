## Controller

The controller for this website is the company named in our [Imprint](/imprint/). You can reach us at [info@elev8x.eu](mailto:info@elev8x.eu).

## Hosting

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you open a page, your browser sends technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. GitHub processes this data to deliver the website and keep it secure. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Details are in GitHub’s privacy statement.

## No cookies, no tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use tracking or advertising tools. Fonts are served from our own domain, so no data is sent to font providers.

## Email enquiries

When you write to us by email, we process your name, email address and the content of your message to answer your enquiry and, where relevant, to prepare a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or because of our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We delete this data when it is no longer needed, unless statutory retention periods apply.

## Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). To use these rights, write to [info@elev8x.eu](mailto:info@elev8x.eu).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for example the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection or the authority in your country of residence.

## Changes

We update this policy when our website or services change. Last updated: 27 November 2026.
