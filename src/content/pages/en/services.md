## Where to start

Pick the decision your team makes most often and with the clearest rules: a carrier choice, an order check, an invoice approval. That is where automation pays back fastest. The free automation check maps that decision, measures what it costs today and gives you a fixed price to automate it.

## What every service includes

- **A baseline before we build**, so the result is measured in hours and errors, not estimated.
- **Guardrails agreed with you**: limits, approvals and fallbacks are set before anything runs on its own.
- **The orchestrator on top**, watching every run and sending your managers one plain-language briefing.
- **EU hosting** under a GDPR data processing agreement, or your own servers.

> If an automation won’t save more than it costs, we tell you before you pay for anything.
