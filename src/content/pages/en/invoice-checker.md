## What changes for the business

- **Overcharges are caught before payment**, not discovered months later.
- **Every invoice is checked**, not a sample, because checking costs the same at any volume.
- **Disputes are quick**, because every mismatch is held with the exact line and reason.
- **Missing documents are chased automatically**, so invoices don’t sit waiting for a CMR.

## Works on top of your TMS

You don’t need to replace your transport management system or buy a separate audit platform. The invoice checker reads invoices from your mailbox or portal, compares them with the orders and agreed rates you already keep, and posts the result where your accounting team works.
