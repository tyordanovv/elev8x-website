## What changes for the business

- **Decisions take minutes, not hours.** With one freight forwarder, the time per enquiry fell from 2 hours to 17 minutes.
- **The same answer every time.** Rules run as code, so the outcome doesn’t depend on who is on shift.
- **Your team works on exceptions and customers** instead of retyping and re-checking.
- **Every decision is logged**: input, rule or model, outcome and who approved it. Audits and disputes take minutes.
- **Costs stay predictable.** Code is cheap to run, and AI is used only where it pays.

## Decisions we automate

- Carrier selection and quote comparison
- Order validation and order entry
- Quote pricing within your margins
- Invoice approval against contract rates
- Reconciliation of deliveries, stock and cash

## Why code first

Code costs almost nothing to run, gives the same answer every time and can be audited line by line. AI is slower, costs money per call and can be wrong, so we use it only where people today read unstructured text or weigh options, and we put firm limits around it. This mix keeps running costs low and results predictable.

## Systems we connect to

Your TMS or ERP, mailboxes, customer portals, freight exchanges such as TIMOCOM, Excel files, and desktop software without an interface, which we operate through its screens.
