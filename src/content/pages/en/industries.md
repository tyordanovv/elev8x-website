## Why these industries

Freight forwarders, carriers and fuel-station groups make the same decisions hundreds of times a week: which carrier, which price, is this order complete, does this invoice match, do these numbers add up. Most of that work follows rules that can be written down. The rest is reading messy emails, PDFs and delivery notes, which is where AI helps.
