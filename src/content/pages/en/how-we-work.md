## Pricing

**A fixed price for the build**, agreed before we start, so the budget can’t drift. After go-live, **one monthly fee** covers monitoring by the digital worker and ongoing improvements: fixes, tuning and small extensions.

> If an automation won’t save more than it costs, we tell you before you pay for anything.

## What we need from you

- One person who knows the process, for two short sessions during the free analysis.
- Examples of the input: the emails, PDFs, exports or screens the process starts from.
- A decision-maker who agrees the limits and approvals before go-live.

## Data and hosting

Everything runs on EU servers under a GDPR data processing agreement. On request, it runs on your own infrastructure. We work in English, German and Bulgarian, both with your team and in the messages the automations send.
