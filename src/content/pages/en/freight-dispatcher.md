## What changes for the business

- **17 minutes of attention per enquiry instead of 2 hours**, measured with a freight forwarder. That is 86% less time per enquiry.
- **More enquiries handled with the same team**, because the routine part no longer needs a person.
- **Faster answers to customers**, because carriers are asked as soon as the request arrives.
- **Margins protected**: every booking stays inside your price limits, and anything above them goes to a person.
- **No retyping**: confirmations and status updates go out without anyone writing them.

## Where the requests come from

Email, PDF attachments, customer portals and freight exchanges. The digital dispatcher reads them in any layout and language, applies your carrier rules, and keeps a record of every enquiry, quote and decision for the orchestrator’s daily briefing.
