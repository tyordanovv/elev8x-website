## What changes for the business

- **Volume grows without hiring for routine work.** A digital role handles the repetitive cases at any hour.
- **Response times drop.** A freight enquiry that took 2 hours now takes 17 minutes.
- **People move to higher-value work**: exceptions, key customers and growth.
- **Every case is documented**, because the orchestrator records each decision and who approved it.

## How a digital role is introduced

1. **Automation check:** we map the role, its decisions and what they cost today.
2. **Pilot with limits:** the digital role takes the routine cases, and your team approves everything outside the limits.
3. **More autonomy as results prove it:** limits widen once the numbers are in, and the orchestrator keeps watching.
