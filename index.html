Why we work this way

We start with the decision, not the model.

Most AI projects begin with a tool and go looking for a problem. We begin with a decision your team makes every day: who makes it, how often, with which information, and what a wrong call costs.

Where the logic is clear, we write it as code. Code is cheaper to run, gives the same answer every time and can be audited line by line. We bring in AI only where people today read messy emails and documents or weigh options, and we put firm limits around it.

Above everything sits one orchestrator. It watches each process and tells your manager, in plain language, what was decided and what needs a person.

How a decision gets made

Every decision takes the cheapest safe path.

Code where the logic is clear, AI where the input is messy, people only for the exceptions.

  1. 01Input

    Input arrives

    Emails, PDFs, portals, spreadsheets or your ERP. Nothing changes for the sender.

  2. 02Code

    Rules check it

    Limits, policies and validation run as plain code. Same input, same result, every time.

  3. 03AI

    AI weighs the messy part

    Only where text is unstructured or options need weighing: reading a request, scoring carriers.

  4. 04Code

    The decision executes

    Inside the guardrails you set, the system acts: it books, posts, approves or replies.

  5. 05Human

    People get the exceptions

    Anything outside the rules lands with the right person, with the full context attached.

The orchestrator records every step: the input, the rule or model that decided, the outcome and who approved it.

The orchestrator

One AI watching every process. One report on your manager’s desk.

As automations multiply, someone has to watch them. The orchestrator is an AI supervisor that checks every run, spots what looks wrong and keeps your managers informed, without another dashboard to babysit.

  • WatchChecks every run, queue and error, around the clock.
  • EscalateSends only real exceptions to the right person, with the context to decide fast.
  • ReportA daily briefing and a weekly summary for the manager, in plain language.
  • ProveAn audit trail for every decision: input, rule or model, outcome.

FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

Do we have to change our software?+

No. We connect to your TMS, ERP, mailbox and portals. Where a system has no interface, we work through its screens.

How is it priced?+

A fixed price for each build, agreed before we start. After go-live, one monthly fee covers monitoring by the orchestrator and ongoing improvements.

What happens when the AI is unsure?+

It does not guess. Every decision has limits, and anything outside them goes to a person with the context attached.

Where does our data live?+

On EU servers, under a GDPR data processing agreement. On request, everything runs on your own infrastructure.

What happens to the people in the role?+

That is your decision. A common path is moving them to exceptions, key customers and growth work while the digital role carries the routine volume.

How soon do we see results?+

The automation check takes one to two weeks. The pilot goes live three to five weeks after that.

Which languages do you work in?+

English, German and Bulgarian, both with your team and in the messages the automations send.
